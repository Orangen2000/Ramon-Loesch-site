Ramón Loesch (b. 2000) lives and works in Vienna.

In my artistic work, I explore human perception within specific systems, time, and the space that surrounds us. I work with the everyday and what lies hidden within it. Contrary to the idea of creating something new or constructing an artificial world into which we can escape, my work instead aims to sharpen our focus on what is already there. I see myself as part of a system of authorship that contributes to the development and experience of an artwork. I constantly strive to reflect anew on which elements I can influence, which influence me, and at what points I make certain decisions or let things take their course.

My works are usually site-specific and engage with their surroundings. Sometimes they blend in, sometimes they challenge the location, and sometimes they extend beyond the site-specific experience. Thus, I understand the individual experience of a place at a specific time in its entirety as the experience of an artwork. It is a process characterized by continuous change. Over time, not only does the outward appearance of a work or its surroundings change, but also the atmosphere it evokes. I am interested in this and constantly try to explore this relationship anew in my work.
